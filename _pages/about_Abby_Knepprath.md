### Abby Knepprath (Master's student)

Abby, originally from Tampa Florida, recently graduated from FGCU in December 2025 with a Bachelor of Science in Biology with a concentration in organismal and ecology. During her undergraduate she studied the vocal and behavioral divergence of Common and Antillean Nighthawks in Southwest Florida and the Florida Keys using auditory playback trials. Following her graduation, she worked a seasonal position with Audubon Florida as a shorebird steward. During her time in this position, she participated in outreach within the community, conservation efforts with state-threatened species, and banding Black Skimmer chicks.  

Her research interests include hybridization and speciation, as well as phylogenetics, conservation biology, and avian population monitoring. For her master’s she is continuing her undergraduate work on Common and Antillean nighthawks, while expanding upon it using genetic sequencing to determine if they are hybridizing in the Keys.
